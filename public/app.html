How it works

1

Build Your Inventory

Add all your furniture pieces with their real dimensions — sofas, beds, desks, shelves, and more. Enter width, depth, and height so every piece is sized accurately.

2

Define Your Rooms

Set up each room with its exact measurements in feet. Mark where the doors and windows are so your layout stays realistic from the start.

3

Design Your Layout

Drag furniture from your inventory onto the floor plan canvas. Rotate pieces, rearrange them freely, and save the layout that works best for you.
